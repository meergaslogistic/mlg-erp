End-to-end data flow

Every operational event follows one path from source to document. Product either enters RPG Stock or moves directly to a trading party. Money is recorded on the party ledger with TID / slip proof.

Level 1 — Origin

Loading source / border / plant
Bowser + Qty (MT)
Rate (now or later)

Level 2 — Choose destination of the gas

Path A — Into company stock

Purchase type: Place in RPG Stock

Writes Purchase register → Inventory IN → Stock voucher PDF

Then sale from stock: Sale register → Inventory OUT → Buyer ledger Debit → Sale voucher PDF

Path B — Direct to party

Purchase type: Direct to party

Writes Purchase register → Party ledger Debit (no inventory) → Purchase voucher PDF

Matching sale (same bowser): Sale register → Buyer ledger Debit → Sale voucher PDF

Level 3 — Settlement of money

Received

Party → MLG bank. Party Credit + company bank Debit. Split across multiple MLG accounts if needed.

Made

MLG → one or many parties. Each payee Debit + MLG bank Credit.

Slip / instructed

One payer → one party, many parties, or many accounts of the same party. Both ledgers per line.

Purchase list
Sale list
Inventory movements

How to brief the client

Product either rests in RPG Stock or is delivered straight to a party. Stock is inventory. Party exposure is the ledger. After every save the system issues a list row plus an A4 voucher; payments also stamp TID and slip number onto both the payment register and the ledger description so the transaction can be traced without leaving the ERP.
